What this does not prove

No independent defect labels, held-out accuracy, false-alarm rate or Teledyne comparison is available. A changed decision is not automatically a better decision. Abstention does not mean the vial is defective.

The recipe labels describe what was requested, not which vials are independently confirmed defective. Both rules reuse the same Run 5 evidence and provisional thresholds.

Adoption and costs

Start in read-only shadow mode. Confirm camera alignment and recipe/time mapping, collect independently reviewed labels, calibrate thresholds, then compare review time and missed defects before any machine integration.

Illustrative planning case, not measured savings: 20 reviews/day × 2 minutes saved × 250 days = 166.7 hours/year. At an assumed $35/hour, that is $5,833/year gross labor value. With assumed $3,000 setup and $1,000/year maintenance, first-year net is $1,833 and simple payback is 7.4 months. At zero minutes saved, first-year net is −$4,000. No scrap reduction is included.

These assumed costs are not quotes. Extra cameras, PLC integration, validation and false-alert handling can increase cost or erase the benefit. Validate these assumptions locally before using this estimate.
